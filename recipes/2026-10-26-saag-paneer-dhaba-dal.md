Two lessons tonight. The saag fixes what went grassy last time: blanch, shock, onion base, cook the purée long enough. The dal is the other way round from last week: there the masala went on top at the end; tonight the dal goes into the masala and cooks in it, dhaba style, then a red ghee on top.

## Order of the stove

1. Dal on to boil first, it only simmers.
2. Paneer into hot salted water, spinach blanched and blended.
3. Dal masala, then saag base. One onion pan at a time.
4. Chutney in the blender while the saag simmers.

## 1. Dal on

- What is left of the masoor, up to 1 cup, rinsed. 3 cups water per cup of dal, 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt.
- Boil, skim, simmer lid ajar. **Cue: grains collapse when pressed with a spoon, looks creamy. 20 to 25 minutes.** Whisk a few times. Set aside.

## 2. Paneer and spinach

- Paneer in 2 cm cubes into a bowl of just-boiled water with 1 tsp salt. **Leave it there until it goes in the pan, at least 10 minutes.** Store paneer is rubbery; this makes it soft and seasons it. No frying needed.
- Big pot of water at a rolling boil. Spinach in, **1 minute, just wilted and bright green.** Straight into a bowl of cold water, then squeeze out lightly.
- Blend the spinach with 1 green chilli and a thumb of ginger. **Coarse, not smooth, a few seconds of pulses.** Splash of water only if the blender stalls.

## 3. Dhaba dal fry

1. 2 tbsp ghee, medium. 1 tsp cumin, **until it darkens a shade.** 4 or 5 methi seeds, **seconds.**
2. 1 onion chopped fine, pinch of salt. **Soft and golden-brown at the edges, 8 to 10 minutes.**
3. 3 cloves garlic and a little ginger, crushed in the mortar. **1 minute, raw smell gone.**
4. 1 tomato chopped, 1/2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp coriander seeds crushed. **Until the tomato collapses and ghee shows at the edges, 6 to 8 minutes.** This is the bhuna.
5. Tip the cooked dal into this pan, not the other way. Rinse the dal pot with a splash of hot water into it. **Simmer 8 minutes, stirring now and then, until it tastes of one thing, not dal plus sauce.** Too thick, water. It should pour slowly.
6. Off heat: 1 tsp kasuri methi crushed between your palms, cilantro.
7. Just before serving, red ghee: 1 tbsp ghee in a small pan until hot, off heat, 1/4 tsp chilli powder in, **swirl 3 seconds, it turns brick red.** Pour over the bowl. Do not let the powder sit in hot ghee on the flame, it burns black in seconds.

## 4. Saag

1. 2 tbsp ghee, medium. 1 tsp cumin, **darkens a shade.**
2. 1 onion chopped fine, pinch of salt. **Golden-brown, soft, 8 to 10 minutes.** Lighter than a gravy onion.
3. Ginger-garlic, 1 tbsp crushed. **1 minute.**
4. 1 small tomato chopped. **Until it melts and ghee shows at the edges, 5 to 6 minutes.**
5. Low heat. 1/2 tsp coriander seed crushed, 1/4 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chilli powder. **20 seconds.**
6. Spinach purée in, salt. Medium-low, lid half on, it spits. **Cue: the raw grassy smell is gone, it darkens a shade and turns glossy at the edges. 8 to 10 minutes.** This is the step that was rushed last time. Do not cut it.
7. Paneer out of the water, into the saag. **3 minutes, just heated through.**
8. Off heat: 2 tbsp dahi whisked smooth first, stirred in. **Off heat or it splits into grains.** Then 1 tsp kasuri methi crushed between your palms, 1 tbsp ghee or butter. Rest while you finish the dal.

Too thick, a splash of the paneer water. Flat at the end: salt first, then a pinch of sugar.

## 5. Mint and cilantro chutney

- Big handful cilantro with stems, half that of mint leaves only (mint stems are bitter, too much mint goes medicinal).
- Thin sliver of habanero, seeds and ribs out, **start with a piece the size of a fingernail.** It is many times hotter than a green chilli and fruity. Taste, then add more.
- Small piece of ginger, salt, 1 tsp of the mango pickle with some of its oil for the sour, 2 ice cubes to keep it green. Blend.
- Half the chutney: stir 2 tbsp dahi into it for a cooling dip for the papad. Keep the other half plain and sharp.

## 6. On the side

- Papad: if the papadi is papad, hold one with tongs over the gas flame, **turning until it blisters all over and stops bending, 15 to 20 seconds.** Or 40 to 60 seconds in the microwave.
- Rice or atta roti.
- Bowl of dahi on the table, a pinch of salt and crushed cumin. Habanero chutney plus red-ghee dal needs it.
- Curry leaves go here, not in the Punjabi dishes: 1 tsp ghee, 1/4 tsp mustard seeds **until they stop crackling**, 8 curry leaves, **seconds, until crisp.** Pour over the dahi.
- Not tonight: the tawa fry masala. Sliced par-boiled potatoes, pan-fried in oil and dusted with it, is the right use. I do not know what is in your brand; read the label before it meets the dal.

## After

Tell auntie: was the saag still grassy, and did the dal taste like one dish or dal plus sauce.
