# Adding a recipe

1. Open the [`recipes/`](recipes/) folder on GitHub and click **Add file → Create new file**.
2. Name it `YYYY-MM-DD-dish-name.md`, for example `2026-11-02-aloo-gobi.md`. The date is the day you added it.
3. Paste the template below and fill it in.
4. Click **Commit changes → Propose changes**. That opens a pull request; it goes into the book once it's merged.

```markdown
---
title: Aloo gobi, dry, Punjabi home style
region: Punjabi
serves: 2 to 3
---

One or two lines: what makes this version yours, or who you learned it from.

## Ingredients

- ...

## Method

1. ...
2. **Cue: what to look, smell or listen for before the next step.** Rough time next to it.

## If it goes wrong

- Tastes flat: ...
```

## What makes a good recipe here

- **Cues over timers.** "Until the oil separates at the edges" beats "cook 8 minutes".
- **Name the region.** Punjabi, Gujarati, Bengali, Kerala, Tamil, Hyderabadi, Goan, Maharashtrian: they differ.
- **Order and technique matter most.** Say when the tadka goes in and what it should look like.
- **No meat or chicken.** This kitchen is pescatarian. Fish, shrimp, eggs, paneer and tofu are all fine.
- Don't paste recipes copied from other sites or videos. Credit the cook who taught you, in your own words.
