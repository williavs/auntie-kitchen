---
title: Tofu and gobi in onion-tomato gravy, with paratha
region: Punjabi home style
date: 2026-10-21
serves: 2, with leftover dal
---

The lesson tonight is the gravy, steps 2 and 4. Together they take 25 to 30 minutes of standing at the stove. If the whole dish took 25 minutes, you skipped the lesson.

Order of work, about 90 minutes: dough, tofu, prep, gravy, dal and rice, paratha, raita.

## 1. Dough first

- 2 cups atta, 1 tsp salt, 1 tbsp ghee rubbed in with your fingers.
- About 3/4 cup warm water, added bit by bit; atta drinks a splash more than plain flour. Knead 5 minutes until smooth and soft. **Softer than you think, like an earlobe.**
- Cover, rest 30 minutes minimum. It can wait longer.

## 2. Tofu

- Press 15 minutes, cube.
- 2 tbsp ghee, medium-high. Fry until **golden on two sides**. Salt in the pan. Set aside.

## 3. Prep for the gravy

- 2 onions, chopped fine.
- 3 tomatoes, chopped fine or blitzed.
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic, grated, equal parts.
- 1 tsp coriander seeds, crushed coarse in the mortar, not to dust.
- Cauliflower in small florets. Green zucchini in thick chunks.
- Optional, for a richer gravy: 10 cashews soaking in hot water while the onions brown. Pound to a paste before step 8. Skip it the first time if you want to taste the plain bhuna.
- 2 green cardamom cracked, 3 cloves, a small piece of cinnamon, 1 tsp cumin seeds.

## 4. The gravy

1. 3 tbsp ghee, medium. Cardamom, cloves, cinnamon in first. **20 seconds, until they smell.** Then the cumin, **until it darkens a shade.**
2. Onions, pinch of salt. Medium, stir now and then. **Cue: deep brown, soft, shrunk to a third, smells sweet. 15 to 20 minutes.** Golden is not done. If they catch, a splash of water, scrape, keep going.
3. Ginger-garlic. **1 minute, until the raw smell goes and the fat looks clear again.** One green chilli, slit.
4. Tomatoes. Medium, stir and scrape. **Cue: collapsed, dark, pulled together into a paste, ghee pooling at the edges when you push it aside. 10 to 12 minutes.** Stop early and it tastes raw and thin. This is the bhuna.
5. Low heat. 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chilli powder (only if the label is plain red chillies, no cumin or oregano), the crushed coriander, salt. **20 seconds.** Then 1 cup hot water.
6. Cauliflower in. Lid on, simmer **6 minutes**.
7. Zucchini in. Lid on, **4 minutes, until both are tender**.
8. Tofu in, cashew paste if using. Lid off, **5 minutes. Gravy should coat a spoon.** Too thin, cook down. Too thick, water.
9. Off heat: 2 tbsp dahi whisked smooth, stirred in. Cilantro. Rest 15 minutes while you do the bread.

## 5. Dal and rice

- Fresh dal tonight: follow the masoor dal sheet (2026-10-20) as written, it landed. Two changes: 4 or 5 methi seeds right after the cumin in tadka step 2, **a shade darker, seconds, more goes bitter**; and 1/4 tsp chilli powder in with the tomato at step 5, never into bare hot ghee. Crush the ginger in the mortar instead of grating.
- Masoor is running out: 3/4 cup dal, 2 1/4 cups water is fine.
- Order of the stove: dal pot on first, it only simmers. Then the gravy onions. Make the dal tadka while the gravy rests at step 9. Never two onion pans at once.
- Rice: sprinkle with water, cover, reheat.

## 6. Paratha

1. Divide dough into 6. Roll one to a 6 inch circle. Brush with ghee, dust with flour, fold in half, then in half again into a triangle. Roll out again, thin.
2. Dry pan, medium-high, no fat. Lay it in. **Cue: small bubbles rise and the underside has pale spots, about 40 seconds.** Flip.
3. Brush the top with ghee, flip, press the edges with a spatula so it puffs. Ghee the other side, flip once more. **Cue: golden brown spots both sides, layers visible at the edge.** About 2 minutes each.
4. Stack under a cloth. Make the rest.

## 7. Raita

- Dahi whisked, salt, chopped mint and cilantro, a pinch of crushed cumin.

## After

Tell auntie: how the gravy tasted (raw, flat, good), and whether the parathas puffed.
