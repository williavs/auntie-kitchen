---
title: Masoor dal with mustard and curry leaf tadka
region: North Indian home style
date: 2026-10-20
serves: 2 to 3
---

Red dal is masoor. It cooks in 20 minutes and does not need soaking. The tadka carries the dish, most of the flavour arrives in the last 90 seconds.

## Dal

1. 1 cup masoor, rinsed. 3 cups water, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp grated ginger. Boil, skim the foam, then simmer with the lid ajar.
2. **Cue: grains collapse when pressed with a spoon, it looks creamy, not watery with grains sitting in it. 20 to 25 minutes.** Whisk a few times at the end to break it down.
3. Too thick, add hot water. **It should pour slowly, not plop.**

## Tadka

Start this when the dal is 5 minutes from done, in a small pan.

1. 3 tbsp ghee, medium-high. When shimmering, 1 tsp mustard seeds. **Wait until they stop crackling.** If they do not pop, the ghee was too cold.
2. Drop to medium. 1 tsp cumin seeds, **5 seconds, until they darken a shade.**
3. 10 curry leaves, stand back, they spit. 1 or 2 green chillies slit, 4 garlic cloves sliced thin. **Cue: garlic edges golden, not brown.** Brown garlic is bitter.
4. One small red onion, chopped fine, pinch of salt. **Soft, edges golden-brown, 6 to 8 minutes.** Dal does not need the deep brown of a gravy.
5. One tomato, chopped. **Until it slumps and the ghee shows again at the edges of the pan, 4 to 5 minutes.** That is the bhuna cue. Do not skip it.
6. Pour the whole pan into the dal. Stir, simmer 3 minutes so they marry.

## Finish, off heat

- Chopped cilantro. Taste for salt.
- Flat? Half a lemon. Sharp from the tomato? A pinch of sugar.
- Rest 20 minutes if you can. Rice or roti.

## Ghee, if making it

One stick unsalted butter, lowest heat, do not stir. It foams and is noisy, then quiets, then the solids at the bottom turn **the colour of a hazelnut skin and it smells like popcorn.** 15 to 20 minutes. Off heat the moment it smells nutty. Strain. Cloudy early on is normal. Dark brown specks and an acrid smell is too far.
